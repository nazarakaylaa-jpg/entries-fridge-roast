## The entry

> My smart fridge updated overnight: the milk expired, the ice maker went on strike, and the app still wants a five-star review.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the one-line roast (126 characters, a single line, punchline at the end) to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it locally with:

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The joke stacks three smart-fridge failures from one overnight firmware update — expired milk, a striking ice maker — and lands on the app nagging for a five-star review of the update that broke everything. No ordering-groceries gag, no watching-you gag: the roast is aimed at the fridge's firmware, its ice maker, and its app.
